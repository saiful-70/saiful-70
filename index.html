CampusQ

Multi-tenant SaaS Live

A coaching centre that ran on paper now runs from one screen, in Bangla.

26 + 12 modulesstudents, batches, attendance, fees, exams, results, SMS, teacher and student portals · operator console for tenants, billing, SMS credits

For
Coaching centres in Bangladesh that run on a paper register, a spreadsheet and the owner's personal mobile-money number. The front desk does the same task eighty times a day.
The call
Build for this niche, not a cut-down school ERP. Bangla first, guardian SMS with a magic link and no account, printed receipts and report cards as first-class output.
Shipped
A multi-tenant SaaS where each centre gets its own subdomain. Plan limits are a designed upgrade state, not an error. Tenant isolation is enforced in Postgres and checked at boot. 2,300+ backend tests, end-to-end coverage over 289 of 303 route cells, business KPIs on Grafana with Telegram alerts.
Cut
The payment gateway (manual transfer plus operator check instead), QR check-in, eight AI features removed in eight slices at the product owner's request, colour palettes from 16 to 6. Removed invented stats from the marketing site before launch. Pre-revenue.

Things I removed

7 cuts

Deciding what not to build is half the job. These were built or planned, then taken out on evidence.

  • Payment gateway integration in CampusQ. Manual transfer plus an operator check was enough for the first customers.
  • Eight AI features in CampusQ, removed in eight slices with no migration.
  • A separate mobile API service at Netpower. The existing module APIs, through the gateway, did the job.
  • PDF input in the AI register importer. Spreadsheets only, so there is no OCR path to maintain.
  • The multi-tenant layer in Rent-ERP. One organisation: no row-level security, no subdomains.
  • Persona switching in Rent-ERP, in favour of plain roles.
  • Fabricated stats and testimonials on the CampusQ marketing site, with a production cleanup script.
